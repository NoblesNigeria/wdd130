Delta State Nigeria

Delta State is an oil and agricultural producing state of Nigeria, situated in the region known as the South-South geopolitical zone, on the northwestern Niger Delta. It is bordered on the north by Edo State, the east by Anambra and Rivers States, and the south by Bayelsa State across the Niger River estuary. The state was formed from the former Bendel State on August 27, 1991. As of 2006, the state had a population of 4,098,391 people. Geographically, Delta State is divided between the Central African mangroves in the coastal southwest and the Nigerian lowland forests in most of the rest of the state as well as the far northeast. The Niger River passes through the state from the northwest to the southeast before emptying into the Gulf of Guinea. The state has a high number of oil wells and large reserves of crude oil and natural gas as one of the main oil-producing states in the country.
